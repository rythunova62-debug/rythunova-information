RythuNova

Making Agricultural Services Easier to Access, Book and Manage

RythuNova is an agricultural services coordination platform designed to help farmers find and arrange the services they need throughout the crop cultivation cycle.

Farmers often depend on personal contacts and repeated phone calls to find tractors, farm machinery, spraying services, labour and harvesting support. RythuNova aims to simplify this process by connecting farmers with local service providers through one accessible coordination platform.

1. Company Overview

Company Name: RythuNova
Industry: Agriculture Technology (AgriTech)
Business Focus: Agricultural Service Discovery and Booking Coordination
Initial Market: Andhra Pradesh, India
Approach: Local agricultural service coordination through phone, WhatsApp and digital technology.

RythuNova's goal is to create a connected platform that supports farmers with essential agricultural services from the beginning of cultivation to harvesting.

2. The Problem

Finding the right agricultural service at the right time can be difficult for farmers.

- Scattered Information: Farmers may have to contact several people to find a suitable service provider.
- Uncertain Availability: Service providers may already be booked, especially during busy cultivation periods.
- Unclear Pricing: Farmers may need to make multiple calls to understand service costs.
- Coordination Difficulties: Delays and cancellations can affect cultivation activities.
- Limited Guidance: New and young farmers may need help identifying the services required at different stages of cultivation.

RythuNova aims to address these challenges by creating a simple and organised service-coordination system.

3. Our Solution

RythuNova provides a local coordination platform where farmers can discover and arrange different agricultural services through one point of contact.

The process works as follows:

1. Farmers contact RythuNova through phone or WhatsApp.
2. RythuNova identifies the required agricultural service.
3. The team contacts suitable local service providers.
4. Provider availability and pricing are checked.
5. The details are shared with the farmer.
6. The booking is confirmed after both parties agree.
7. RythuNova follows up after service completion and collects feedback.

This approach helps reduce the effort required to find and coordinate agricultural services.

4. Agricultural Services

RythuNova aims to coordinate services across the complete cultivation cycle, including:

- Land preparation and tractor services.
- Agricultural machinery and equipment rental.
- Sowing and planting services.
- Spraying services.
- Agricultural labour coordination.
- Harvesting and related services.

Additional services may be introduced based on farmer requirements and local service-provider availability.

5. Target Customers

Farmers

Our primary customers are small and medium farmers who require external agricultural services and may not own all the machinery or equipment needed for cultivation.

Agricultural Service Providers

RythuNova also works with local tractor owners, machinery operators, spraying operators, labour coordinators and harvesting service providers.

Our aim is to connect farmers and service providers through a reliable coordination process.

6. What Makes RythuNova Different?

- Local-first Approach: Starting with individual villages and understanding farmers' actual needs.
- One Coordination Point: Helping farmers discover multiple cultivation services through one platform.
- Assisted Booking: Farmers can request services through phone calls or WhatsApp.
- Clear Communication: Checking availability, sharing prices and confirming bookings.
- Service Follow-up: Collecting feedback and tracking service completion.

RythuNova aims to build trust through reliable local service coordination.

7. Business Model

RythuNova is exploring different revenue opportunities to build a sustainable business.

Potential revenue models include:

- Coordination fees for completed bookings.
- Service commissions from participating providers.
- Provider subscription or service-partner plans.

The final revenue model will depend on farmer willingness to pay, provider participation and operating costs.

- Booking management system.

Future Technology

9. Initial Market Approach

RythuNova plans to begin its operations in one pilot village in Andhra Pradesh.

The initial focus will be on:

1. Understanding farmers' service requirements.
2. Identifying frequently needed agricultural services.
3. Building a local service-provider network.
4. Testing the booking and coordination process.
5. Collecting farmer and provider feedback.
6. Improving the platform before expanding to more villages.

West Godavari and East Godavari are possible areas for initial market exploration.

11. Growth and Future Development

RythuNova aims to grow step by step.

Phase 1: Build a local network of farmers and agricultural service providers.

Phase 2: Test agricultural service discovery and booking coordination.

Phase 3: Develop a digital platform for managing farmers, providers and bookings.

Phase 4: Introduce suitable IoT-based technology and additional agricultural support services.

12. Measuring Success

RythuNova will measure its progress through:

- Number of farmer service requests.
- Number of confirmed bookings.
- Number of completed services.
- Provider response time.
- Booking fulfilment rate.
- Farmer satisfaction.
- Repeat customer usage.
- Growth of the local service-provider network.

These measurements will help improve the service and guide future business decisions.

13. Our Vision

To make essential agricultural services easier for every farmer to access, coordinate and use—from the beginning of cultivation to harvesting.

RythuNova aims to become a trusted agricultural services platform that connects farmers with service providers and supports a more organised cultivation-service ecosystem.
